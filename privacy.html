ClipperMax

Privacy policy

Last updated 16 November 2026

This policy covers the ClipperMax upload app ("the app"): a private tool that the owner of the ClipperMax channels runs on their own computer to post the channels' own videos to the ClipperMax accounts on YouTube, Instagram and Facebook.

Who uses the app

Only the owner of the ClipperMax channels. The app is not offered to the public and nobody else can sign in to it.

What the app accesses

When the owner signs in, the app receives permission to upload videos to the owner's YouTube channel and to read that channel's name and identifier, and permission to publish videos to the owner's Instagram account and Facebook Page. It uses these permissions only to post the owner's videos and to confirm which account it is connected to.

What the app collects

Nothing about viewers, followers or any other person. The app does not collect, store, sell or share personal data. The sign-in tokens that let it post are kept only on the owner's computer and are never sent anywhere except to YouTube and Meta to make the uploads.

YouTube and Meta services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it, the owner agrees to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be removed at any time from the Google security settings page. Posts to Instagram and Facebook are made through Meta's platform, covered by the Meta Privacy Policy.

Deleting data

Because the app holds no personal data about anyone but its owner, there is nothing to delete for other people. The owner can delete the stored sign-in tokens at any time by removing them from their computer and revoking the app's access in their Google and Meta account settings.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or requests to remove a clip, can be sent by direct message to @clippermaxhq on Instagram.
